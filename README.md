# Bishoy Nageuib - Professional Portfolio & CV

A modern, responsive, bilingual (Arabic & English) React application showcasing the professional portfolio, CV, academic credentials, and certifications of **Bishoy Nageuib George** — Financial Accountant, Banking & Customer Service Specialist, and Administrator.

## Features

- **Bilingual Support (English & Arabic)**: Instant language switching with dynamic document direction (`ltr` / `rtl`).
- **Dark & Light Mode**: Accessible, eye-friendly theme toggle with persistent preferences in local storage.
- **Hero & Profile Summary**: Professional portrait, headline, career summary, quick section navigation pills, and instant PDF CV download.
- **Open to Work Banner**: Direct highlight of target opportunities in financial accounting, banking, and administration.
- **Academic Qualifications**: DBA candidacy at Tanta University, MBA in Finance & Investment (Grade: Excellent), and BIS Bachelor Degree (Class Rank: 4th, 90%).
- **Work Experience Timeline**: Structured timeline of concurrent and past roles with bulleted accomplishments.
- **Structured Skills Matrix**: 4 key domains (Accounting & Finance, Banking & Customer Service, Administrative & Operations, and Software/Digital Tools).
- **Certificates & Courses**: Full breakdown of 12 verified qualifications including TOEFL preparation, Digital Transformation (FDTC), Banque Misr operations, PFA, SAP ERP, Oracle Financial ERP, and Odoo ERP.
- **Interactive Documents Gallery**: High-resolution image preview modal with keyboard/overlay controls and direct download capability.
- **Contact & Communication**: Contact methods (Email, LinkedIn, Wuzzuf, Phone, WhatsApp, Address) and mailto contact form.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React 19 + TypeScript
- **Bundler**: Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS & Custom Theme System
- **Typography & Icons**: Cairo, Inter, Font Awesome 6
